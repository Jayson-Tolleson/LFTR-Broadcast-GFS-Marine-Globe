LFTR Broadcast/GFS SST hotfix

Problem addressed:
- Ocean SST could disappear when the HYCOM NCSS surface bundle did not return a usable `sst` grid.
- Bait, boats, shark intel, and hover water temperature need SST even if live current vectors are missing or HYCOM changes/stalls.

Fix included:
- Keep HYCOM NCSS as the primary ocean/current source.
- Add NOAA OISST ERDDAP observed-SST fallback when HYCOM SST is empty.
- Keep current fallback strict; this does not fake live currents.
- Add source_meta.sst_source and source_meta.sst_fallback diagnostics.
- Bump HYCOM cache schema to avoid stale empty-SST cache hits after install.

Useful logs to watch:
- "oisst erddap SST fallback used"
- "hycom subset empty"
- source_meta.sst_source = "oisst_erddap_fallback" or "hycom_ncss"
