LFTR viewport projection + stability patch

- Camera bbox is now heading/tilt projected instead of centered directly under the camera.
- Fallback bbox shoots the visible data footprint forward along camera heading.
- Giant world-start ocean bboxes are capped to a frustum-sized request so HYCOM does not serve a coarse unrelated middle slice.
- Service restart policy is set to Restart=always so clean hypercorn exits do not leave the app down.
- Runner exports malloc/HDF/single-worker compatibility vars.
